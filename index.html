CONTROLS & MATCH RULES

Enter to start. Click the game to capture your mouse. Escape or P pauses. R reloads; 1/2 switch rifle and shotgun. Z/X/C select a wall, ramp or floor; Q or right click places it for 20 materials. Collect glowing health, shield and material pickups. The purple storm shrinks toward the island center. Outlast 15 bots to win.

Desktop keyboard and mouse required. This is an original local single-player prototype. Use Fullscreen for the best view.
